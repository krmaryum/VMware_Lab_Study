English questions/options with English/Roman Urdu explanations after submission. Timer: 20 minutes, 30 minutes or untimed. Correct one point, unanswered zero, no negative marking. Timeout auto-submits. Reattempt resets and shuffles questions/options. Incorrect/unanswered review and light print layout included. Refresh clears the attempt; no data uploaded.

The 10000/20000 Kbit/s and 1024 KB settings are proposed exercise values. They are not recorded benchmark results or recommendations to change management policy. Peak is a ceiling, not a per-interval guarantee. Actual test paths, units and effective policies must be checked.
