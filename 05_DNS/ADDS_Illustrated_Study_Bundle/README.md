# AD DS - Illustrated Study Bundle

Complete English and Roman Urdu notes based on the supplied 22-page lecture, with original lecture visuals and corrected educational diagrams.

## Start here

1. Read [English notes](ADDS_Complete_Book_Based_Study_Notes_English.md) or [Roman Urdu notes](ADDS_Complete_Book_Based_Study_Notes_Roman_Urdu.md).
2. Review the seven embedded English diagrams alongside their explanations.
3. Use the correction register when reviewing the 11 original lecture-page images in the appendix.
4. Review the 30 questions and answers.
5. Read [image provenance and sources](IMAGES_AND_SOURCES.md).

Asal tafseel short nahin ki gayi. Dono notes mein 18 chapters, tables, commands, corrections aur 30 revision jawab barqarar hain. Naye diagrams English mein hain; captions aur notes apni apni zaban mein hain.
