# Images and Sources

## Original lecture images

The 11 PNGs in `images/source_pages/` are page renders of the learner-supplied 22-page PDF, `09 Active Directory Domain Service (ADDS)(3).pdf` (PDF pages 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15, 17, 18, 20 and 22). No source page wording was rewritten. Existing diagrams and statements may be simplified or inaccurate; the notes explain corrections. Repeated decorative chapter-divider pages are mapped in the notes rather than included as extra images.

These are supplied lecture excerpts, not claimed as original artwork or official Microsoft screenshots. Publication/reuse permission for teacher material must come from its rights holder; no licence is invented here.
